Parameter descriptions for data/params.csv
==========================================

These parameters are summary statistics derived from Imaris segmentation outputs
by readEmbryos.R and readPheno.R.

Cell types:
  - TVC: Trunk Ventral Cells (cardiopharyngeal progenitors)
  - ATM: Anterior Tail Muscles

Suffixes:
  - _Nucleus: computed from nucleus centroid coordinates
  - _Cell: computed from cell centroid coordinates
  - min/max/mean/sd or SD: summary statistics across cells within each embryo

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISTANCE PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ATM.dist.mean_{Nucleus,Cell}
  Mean pairwise distance between ATM cells, normalized by maximum distance.

TVC.dist.{min,max,mean,sd}_{Nucleus,Cell}
  Pairwise distances between TVC cells, normalized by maximum distance.

TVC.ATM.dist.{min,max,mean,sd}_{Nucleus,Cell}
  Distances between TVC and ATM cells, normalized by maximum distance.

TVC.cosine.{min,max,sd}_{Nucleus,Cell}
  Cosine of angles formed by triplets of TVC cells. Measures angular
  arrangement/spread of TVC cells. Values near 1 indicate aligned cells,
  values near -1 indicate cells at opposite ends.

ATM.Nucleus.Dist.to.Cell.Membrane.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Distance from ATM nucleus centroid to cell membrane, normalized.

TVC.Nucleus.Dist.to.Cell.Membrane.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Distance from TVC nucleus centroid to cell membrane, normalized.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CELL SHAPE PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{ATM,TVC}.Cell.Ellipticity..oblate..{min,max,mean,SD}
  Oblate ellipticity of cells (0-1). Higher values indicate disc/pancake shape.

{ATM,TVC}.Cell.Ellipticity..prolate..{min,max,mean,SD}
  Prolate ellipticity of cells (0-1). Higher values indicate cigar/elongated shape.

{ATM,TVC}.Cell.Sphericity.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Sphericity of cells (0-1). Value of 1 indicates perfect sphere.

{ATM,TVC}.Cell.Volume.frac.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Fraction of total cell volume occupied by each cell type.

{ATM,TVC}.Cell.Nucleus.to.Cytoplasm.Volume.Ratio.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Ratio of nucleus volume to cytoplasm volume for each cell.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NUCLEUS SHAPE PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{ATM,TVC}.Nucleus.Ellipticity..oblate..{min,max,mean,SD}
  Oblate ellipticity of nuclei.

{ATM,TVC}.Nucleus.Ellipticity..prolate..{min,max,mean,SD}
  Prolate ellipticity of nuclei.

{ATM,TVC}.Nucleus.Sphericity.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Sphericity of nuclei.

{ATM,TVC}.Nucleus.Volume.frac.{min,max,mean,SD}
  Fraction of total nucleus volume occupied by each cell type.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CELL COUNTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

nATM
  Number of ATM cells in the embryo.

nTVC
  Number of TVC cells in the embryo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GROUP/SURFACE PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

{ATM,TVC}group.Ellipticity..oblate.
  Oblate ellipticity of the grouped surface for ATM/TVC.

{ATM,TVC}group.Ellipticity..prolate.
  Prolate ellipticity of the grouped surface for ATM/TVC.

{ATM,TVC}group.Sphericity
  Sphericity of the grouped surface for ATM/TVC.

{ATM,TVC}.contiguous
  Whether cells form a single contiguous surface (1) or multiple surfaces (0).

{ATM,TVC}.Volume
  Fractional volume of the ATM/TVC group surface.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMAGING PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Threshold.nuclei
  Intensity threshold used for nuclei segmentation in Imaris.

Threshold.membrane
  Intensity threshold used for membrane segmentation in Imaris.

surface.dist
  Shortest edge-to-edge distance between ATM group and TVC group surfaces (um).
